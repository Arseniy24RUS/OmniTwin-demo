# Public fictional population v2

This pure browser/Node module describes a **fictional** city. It is not the research model, a forecast, or individual-level observed data. The old demo remains unchanged.

`tools/build-city-population-v2.mjs` reads the reviewed public 2024 Chelyabinsk age/sex aggregate and pinned `city-v2` geography. It generates a 2026 baseline of 1,177,058 weight=1 residents with exact 21-band sex marginals. The transfer from 2024 reference to a fictional 2026 baseline is explicit. Single ages within bands, ages 100–105 for the open tail, household relationships, occupations, placement, and all 2027–2036 events are authored assumptions. They are not calibrated probabilities or observed occupancy.

## Manifest and binary contracts

`apps/web/public/demo-v2/manifest.json` identifies immutable SHA-256/byte-counted assets. URLs are relative to the manifest. Clients must pin/verify the manifest and verify fetched assets. Person shards contain at most 8,192 rows; household shards contain at most 4,096 households. No biographies are stored. `profileFor` derives the same canonical fictional text on browser/server from a verified record, active household size, district and scenario/year. Client traits must never override server persona.

All integers are little-endian. Both headers are 32 bytes: eight uint32 values `[magic, 2, startIndex, count, stride, memberCount, 0, 0]`. Person magic is `0x32504d4f`, household magic `0x32484d4f`.

Person stride is 16 bytes: householdIndex u32; birthYear u16; sex u8 (0 male, 1 female); role/entry u8 (low nibble head/partner/child/other = 0/1/2/3, high nibble initial/birth/immigration = 0/1/2); scenario mask u8 (baseline/inflow/ageing = bits 1/2/4); entry year offset u8 from 2026; three scenario exit offsets u8 (0 alive, 1–10 = 2027–2036); three corresponding exit reasons u8 (0 none, 1 death, 2 emigration). Baseline people are stored **once**, mask 7. Scenario-specific entries have one mask bit. A person is active when included, entered by selected year, and exit is absent or strictly later. `demo2-p-0000000` IDs encode the global zero-based row number.

Household stride is metadata8: homeBuildingIndex u32 (`0xffffffff` unplaced), district ordinal u8 (`255` unknown), three reserved zero bytes. Then uint32 CSR offsets[count+1] and uint32 global member indices[memberCount]. Members include all scenario entries and must be filtered for selected scenario/year. Whole household home assignment is stable; district ordering is exported as `DISTRICT_IDS`. Placement tries deterministic weighted candidates but accepts only whole households fitting `max(1,floor(capacityWeight))` in all 33 scenario/year states. This is a hard **synthetic** capacity, not an observed occupancy. Exhausted capacity fails compilation instead of silently overfilling buildings. Unknown-use or unresolved-district buildings never receive inferred residential capacity. Building ordinal refers to the exact pinned city-v2 global index, not visible-cell ordering.

Summaries contain 264 snapshots (3 scenarios × 11 years × city and 7 districts), exact coarse and 21-band age/sex counts, employment, active household counts, the 50-cell ageBand×sex×employment cube, and events in the preceding annual interval. Every district and city stock satisfies previous + births − deaths + immigration − emigration, with no internal moves. Future events are illustrative scaled legacy scenario counts; this is not free-running validation.

The small sparse `queryIndex` lists per-person-shard counts for each scenario/year, keyed by `districtIndex*50 + ageBandIndex*10 + sexIndex*5 + employmentIndex`. It permits exact page offsets and cohort totals without loading all person shards. The v2 browser accepts exact person IDs as its search contract; it does not pretend a bounded sample is a complete name search. Unknown household counts in cohort slices or unprepared list profiles remain `null`. A selected profile resolves its complete bounded household membership before chat.

Run codec/compiler fixtures with `node --test shared/demo-population/test/*.test.mjs`. Compile in one low-priority process with `node --max-old-space-size=384 --v8-pool-size=1 tools/build-city-population-v2.mjs`; the CLI adjusts only its own priority and reports peak RSS. Full build requires the source-backed city-v2 index and fails on integrity, marginal or conservation mismatch. No external requests or research paths are used.
